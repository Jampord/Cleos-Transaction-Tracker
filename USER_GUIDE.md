# User guide

A plain-language guide for the person who runs the shop and uses the app every day.

## The big picture

The app does the same job as the notebook: you write down each GCash transaction, load sale and water sale. The difference is that it works out the fees, shows how much you earned today, remembers who owes you money, and tells you if the cash in the drawer is not what it should be.

It works **without internet**. Everything stays on your phone.

## The four tabs at the bottom

| Tab            | What it is for                                      |
| -------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| **Today**      | Record entries, see today's profit and sales        |
| **Utang**      | See who owes you money and record payments          |
| **Water**      | See how many gallons are left and record deliveries |
| **Cash check** | Count the drawer and GCash at the end of the day    |

At the top right of every screen: the **moon / sun** switches between dark and light, and the **gear** opens Settings.

## Recording a transaction

On **Today**, tap one of the four big buttons.

### Cash-in

The customer gives you cash and you send them GCash.

1. Type the **amount**. The **fee** appears by itself.
2. Type the customer's **name or number** (optional). Names you used before appear as buttons you can tap.
3. Choose **Paid** or **Unpaid**.
4. The box **"At the counter"** shows how much the customer pays you.
5. Tap **Save**.

### Cash-out

The customer sends you GCash and you give them cash.

1. Type the **amount**. The fee appears by itself.
2. Choose how the fee is handled:
   - **Fee on top**: the customer pays the fee in addition.
   - **Fee deducted**: you give the amount **minus** the fee.
3. The box **"At the counter"** shows exactly how much cash to give.
4. Tap **Save**.

### Load

Type the amount. Small loads get a fee automatically. You can change the fee on any entry.

### Water sale

Type how many **gallons** and the **price per gallon** (it is filled in for you). Tap **Save**.

## When a customer has not paid (utang)

Choose **Unpaid** and type the customer's **name**. The app needs the name to keep track of who owes what.

To record a payment later:

1. Go to the **Utang** tab and tap the customer.
2. Type the amount received.
3. Choose **Cash** or **GCash**, depending on how they paid.
4. Tap **Save payment**, or **Pay in full** if they paid everything.

A payment goes to the customer's **oldest** entry first. If they say the money is for something specific, tap that entry first: it turns blue, and the payment goes to that one.

If you typed a wrong payment, open the entry from **Today** (go to that day with the arrows) and tap **Remove** next to the payment.

## Water

On the **Water** tab, tap **Add delivery** when water arrives. The app then knows how many gallons you have. Every sale takes gallons away, and **Water left** is always on the Today screen.

If it says you sold more than you have, you may have forgotten to record a delivery. You can still save the sale.

## Fixing a mistake

- Tap any entry on **Today** to change it.
- To remove one, open it and tap **Delete entry**.
- Deleted something by mistake? **Settings → Deleted entries → Restore**.
- An entry that already has payments cannot be changed (except the name) until you remove those payments.
- To see an earlier day, use the **◀ ▶** arrows at the top of the Today screen.

## The end of the day: Cash check

1. The **first time**, the app asks for your starting balances. Count the **cash** and check your **GCash balance**, and type both.
2. Later, at closing time, open **Cash check**. It shows how much cash and GCash you **should** have.
3. Count the drawer, check GCash, and type the real amounts.
4. A green **Matches** means everything agrees. **Short** or **Over** shows the difference.
5. Tap **Save count**. The next check starts from your numbers.

If there is a difference, look at the day's entries for something forgotten, a wrong amount or a payment recorded with the wrong method (Cash or GCash).

## Backups: please do this every week

Your records only exist on this phone. If the phone is lost or broken, so are they, unless you have a backup.

1. **Settings → Backup and restore → Back up now.**
2. Send the file to your email, Google Drive or Messenger, and keep it there.

The Today screen shows a red reminder when it has been a week.

## Please remember

- **Never uninstall the app.** That deletes every record. If the app needs an update, install the new version **over** the old one, after a backup.
- If the phone is replaced, the person who looks after the app can put your records on the new one from your latest backup.
- Don't share the backup file with anyone else. It contains your customers' names.

## Common questions

**The fee looks wrong.** Fees come from the table in **Settings → GCash fee table**. You can change any fee there. A change applies to new entries only.

**The profit does not match my notebook.** Profit counts the fees on GCash, the earnings on load, and the earnings on water (selling price minus what you pay the supplier). Check that the water price and cost in Settings are right.

**Can I use dark mode?** Yes: the moon icon at the top, or **Settings → Appearance** (Light, Dark or Automatic, which follows the phone).
